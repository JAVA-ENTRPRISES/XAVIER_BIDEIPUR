About St. Xavier's School

At St. Xavier's School, Bideipur, we blend rigorous academics with values-based education. Our mission is to develop well-rounded individuals who lead with wisdom, courage, and compassion.

Our Mission

To provide quality education rooted in Christian values, fostering intellectual curiosity, moral integrity, and social responsibility in every student.

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution in Bhadrak district, recognised for academic excellence, character formation, and community service.

Our Values

Faith, discipline, respect, and service guide our community—building strong character, meaningful relationships, and a lifelong love of learning.
